Privacy Policy — TextRec.

Last updated: November 5, 2026

Data controller: Batuhan Savaş Yılmaz (From B.S.Y.) — batuhansavasyilmaztr@gmail.com — https://github.com/batuhansavas-yilmaz

1. Scope

This policy applies to the TextRec. web application at batuhansavas-yilmaz.github.io/textrecweb and the TextRec. Android app (com.bsavasy.textrec). Both are operated by the same data controller and share this policy.

2. How your content is processed

In the Web App, photos and PDF files you select are processed only in your browser using PaddleOCR and PDF.js. In the Android app, photos and PDF pages are processed on your device using Google ML Kit. In both cases, your photos, PDFs, OCR text, and generated output are not uploaded to us, Firebase, or any other server. You can save results as .txt or .docx; PDF export is not available. When you use the system share sheet, the file is sent only to the app you choose.

3. Firebase services

  • Firebase Analytics (Web App and Android app): screen views, feature usage, and non-content events; app/device information. We do not send photos, PDFs, OCR text, or file names.
  • Firebase Performance Monitoring (Web App and Android app): page load, model download, OCR/PDF processing, export and network performance metrics. No content is included.
  • Firebase Remote Config (Web App and Android app): fetches configuration values such as feature flags. It does not receive your content.
  • Firebase Crashlytics (Android app only — Not available in the Web App): crash reports, stack traces, and device/app state used to diagnose stability issues. We do not log photos, PDFs, OCR text, or file names.
  • Consent: Firebase Analytics is enabled only after you accept the analytics consent banner. On the Web App your choice is stored in localStorage; on the Android app it is stored in local app preferences. If you decline, Analytics stays disabled.

4. Local storage

In the Web App, small preferences (OCR language, model mode, analytics consent) are stored in localStorage. A service worker caches app files and OCR models on your device; it does not cache your photos, PDFs, or OCR text.

In the Android app, preferences such as analytics consent are stored in the app's local preferences. Photos, PDFs, OCR text, and exported files remain on your device; the app does not keep a cloud or in-app history.

5. Hosting logs

The Web App is served by GitHub Pages and Firebase Hosting (Not available in the Mobile App). These providers may process IP address, user agent, requested URL, timestamps, and security/error logs to deliver and protect the service. The Android app does not use Firebase Hosting.

6. Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal data. We do not store your photos, PDFs, or OCR text. Local settings, cached files, and the web service worker cache remain on your device until you clear site data or app data. Analytics data follows Google Analytics retention (2 or 14 months); hosting logs follow GitHub and Firebase policies; Crashlytics reports follow Firebase retention policies.

7. Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, and withdrawal of consent. On the Web App you can clear local data by clearing browser site data; on the Android app you can clear app data. To withdraw Analytics consent, clear the app's or site's local data and choose Decline on the next launch. For requests, contact us. You may complain to your local data protection authority (KVKK in Turkey or your EU supervisory authority).

8. International transfers, children and security

Google Firebase and GitHub may process technical data outside your country under appropriate safeguards. TextRec is not directed to children under 13. The Web App uses HTTPS; OCR processing is local. The Android app processes OCR on-device and does not upload your content.
