1. Scope

This policy applies to the TextRec. web application at batuhansavas-yilmaz.github.io/textrecweb. The Android app has a separate policy.

2. How your content is processed

Photos and PDF files you select are processed only in your browser using PaddleOCR and PDF.js. They are not uploaded to us, Firebase, or any other server. OCR text and generated output stay on your device.

You can save the result as .txt or .docx. PDF export is not available on the web. When you use the system share sheet, the file is sent only to the app or service you choose; we do not receive it.

3. Local storage and service worker

  • Small preferences (OCR language and model mode) are stored in browser localStorage.
  • A service worker caches app files and OCR models on your device; it does not cache your photos, PDFs, or OCR text.
  • No advertising cookies are used.

4. Hosting logs

The app is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process technical data such as IP address, user agent, requested URL, timestamps, and security/error logs to deliver and protect the service. GitHub's retention periods and privacy terms apply.

5. What we do not use

We do not use accounts, advertising, Firebase Analytics, Crashlytics, or Performance Monitoring in the web app. We do not sell personal data.

6. Retention

We do not store your photos, PDFs, or OCR text. Local settings and cached files remain on your device until you clear site data. Hosting logs are retained by GitHub according to its policies.

7. Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, and withdrawal of consent where applicable. You can clear all local data by clearing your browser's site data. For requests, contact us. You may also complain to your local data protection authority (KVKK in Turkey or your EU supervisory authority).

8. International transfers

GitHub Pages may process technical data outside your country under GitHub's safeguards.

9. Children

TextRec is not directed to children under 13 (or the applicable age in your country). We do not knowingly collect data from children.

10. Security

The site is served over HTTPS. OCR processing is local. We do not operate a server that stores your content.

11. Changes

We may update this policy. The latest version will be posted on this page with a new “Last updated” date.
