Last updated: November 5, 2026

Data controller: Batuhan Savaş Yılmaz (From B.S.Y.) — batuhansavasyilmaztr@gmail.com — https://github.com/batuhansavas-yilmaz

1. Scope

This policy applies to the TextRec. web application at batuhansavas-yilmaz.github.io/textrecweb. The Android app has a separate policy.

2. How your content is processed

Photos and PDF files you select are processed only in your browser using PaddleOCR and PDF.js. They are not uploaded to us, Firebase, or any other server. OCR text and generated output stay on your device. You can save the result as .txt or .docx; PDF export is not available on the web. When you use the system share sheet, the file is sent only to the app you choose.

3. Analytics, Performance and Remote Config

  • Firebase Analytics: screen views, feature usage and non-content events; app/device information. We do not send photos, PDFs, OCR text, or file names.
  • Firebase Performance Monitoring: page load, model download and network performance metrics.
  • Firebase Remote Config: fetches configuration values (for example feature flags); it does not receive your content.
  • A/B Testing: experiments use consented Analytics data and Remote Config.
  • Consent: analytics cookies are enabled only after you accept the in-app consent banner. If you decline, Analytics stays disabled.

4. Local storage and service worker

Small preferences (OCR language and model mode) are stored in localStorage. A service worker caches app files and OCR models on your device; it does not cache your photos, PDFs, or OCR text.

5. Hosting logs

The app is served by GitHub Pages. GitHub may process IP address, user agent, requested URL, timestamps, and security/error logs to deliver and protect the service.

6. Sharing and retention

We do not sell personal data. We do not store your photos, PDFs, or OCR text. Local settings and cached files remain on your device until you clear site data. Analytics data follows Google Analytics retention (2 or 14 months); hosting logs follow GitHub policies.

7. Your rights

You may request access, correction, deletion, restriction, objection, and withdrawal of consent. You can clear local data by clearing browser site data. For requests, contact us. You may complain to your local data protection authority (KVKK in Turkey or your EU supervisory authority).

8. International transfers, children and security

Google Firebase and GitHub may process technical data outside your country under appropriate safeguards. TextRec is not directed to children under 13. The site uses HTTPS; OCR processing is local.
